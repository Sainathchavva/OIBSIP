# VERDORA — Professional Logo Design

## Task 2: Logo Design

### 1. Project Overview

VERDORA is a fictional technology brand created as part of the Graphics Design internship project.

The objective of this task is to design a professional, minimalist, and scalable logo that communicates the brand's identity through a simple visual symbol, intentional typography, and a consistent colour palette.

The logo represents the idea of combining technology, growth, innovation, and sustainability to create a better future.

---

## 2. Brand Information

**Brand Name:** VERDORA

**Industry:** Technology / Innovation

**Brand Concept:** Technology for a brighter future

**Tagline:**  
> TECHNOLOGY FOR A BRIGHTER YOU

---

## 3. Logo Concept

The VERDORA logo combines a stylized letter **"V"** with leaf-inspired shapes.

The "V" represents the brand name VERDORA, while the leaf elements represent growth, sustainability, development, and positive change.

The combination of the geometric "V" and organic leaf forms creates a balance between technology and nature.

The design follows a minimalist approach so that the logo remains recognizable and usable across different sizes and platforms.

---

## 4. Design Philosophy

The logo follows the **KISS principle — Keep It Simple**.

The design avoids unnecessary details and uses a small number of colours and simple shapes. This makes the logo suitable for websites, mobile applications, social media, business cards, presentations, and other digital or print applications.

---

## 5. Colour Palette

The logo uses a modern blue, teal, and green colour palette.

| Colour | Hex Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | `#0EA5E9` | Technology, trust, reliability |
| Teal | `#10B981` | Growth, innovation, freshness |
| Green | `#18E38A` | Sustainability, progress |
| Dark Navy | `#0F172A` | Professionalism and stability |

The gradient combination creates a modern technology-focused appearance while maintaining a connection with nature and growth.

---

## 6. Typography

The wordmark uses a clean geometric sans-serif style.

**Primary Typeface:** Poppins

The typography was selected because it provides:

- Clean appearance
- Good readability
- Modern visual identity
- Professional appearance
- Strong compatibility with digital platforms

The wordmark uses uppercase letters to provide a strong and confident brand presence.

---

## 7. Logo Variations

### Reversed Version

`VERDORA_reversed.png`

---

## 8. Vector Version

`VERDORA.svg`

The SVG version provides a scalable vector representation of the logo.

Vector graphics can be resized without the same pixelation limitations as raster images, making the logo suitable for different screen and print sizes.
